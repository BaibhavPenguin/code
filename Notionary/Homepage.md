## Overview
---
**Notionary** is my personal workspace and archive for studying more efficiently without relying on pen and paper all the time, It its completely serverless and edge cached. It features web hosted versions of Python, R and MySQL via Pyodide, WebR and Lite4MariaDB with more coming soon!
