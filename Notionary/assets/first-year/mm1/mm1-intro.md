## <u>Introduction to Marketing and Marketing Management</u>
---

### What is Marketing?
In simple terms, **marketing** is the process of creating, communicating, delivering, and exchanging offerings that have value for customers, clients, partners, and society at large. 

### Marketing vs. Selling
*   **Selling** focuses on the needs of the seller (converting product into cash).
*   **Marketing** focuses on the needs of the buyer (satisfying customer needs through the product and its delivery).

### What is Marketing Management?
**Marketing Management** is the art and science of choosing target markets and getting, keeping, and growing customers through creating, delivering, and communicating superior customer value.

It involves five key steps:
1. **Analyzing** market opportunities (finding customer pain points).
2. **Selecting** target markets (identifying who needs the solution).
3. **Designing** marketing strategies (positioning the product).
4. **Planning** marketing programs (the 4 Ps).
5. **Organizing, Implementing, and Controlling** the marketing effort.

## <u> Concepts of Marketing</u>
---

### Need, Want, and Demand
*   **Need:** Basic human requirement (e.g., communication, security, transportation).
*   **Want:** A specific form that a need takes, directed by culture and individual personality (e.g., the need to communicate becomes a want for an iPhone or WhatsApp).
*   **Demand:** Wants for specific products backed by an ability and willingness to pay (e.g., wanting a high-end gaming laptop and having the money to buy it).

### Target Market, Positioning, and Segmentation (STP)
*   **Segmentation:** Grouping a diverse market into smaller, homogenous segments (e.g., students, gamers, enterprise businesses).
*   **Targeting:** Choosing which segment(s) to focus on.
*   **Positioning:** Creating a distinct mental image and value proposition of your product in the target customer's mind relative to competitors (e.g., *Slack* positioned as the workplace communication hub).

### Customer Value and Satisfaction
*   **Value:** The customer's perception of the benefits of a product versus the cost paid to obtain it 

*   **Satisfaction:** A person's feelings of pleasure or disappointment resulting from comparing a product's perceived performance to their expectations.

## <u>Product Mix</u>

**Product Mix** is the complete set of all products or items that a specific company offers for sale.

### Key concepts of Product Mix
A product mix consists of four key dimensions:

1. **Width:** The number of different product lines the company carries.
2. **Length:** The total number of items within all product lines.
3. **Depth:** The number of variants (size, color, features, versions) offered for each product in a line.
4. **Consistency:** How closely related the various product lines are in end-use, production, distribution channels, or target markets.
